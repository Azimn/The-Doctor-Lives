# Eidolon E4-C — Frozen-feature balanced readout, source-protocol v0.1

**Status:** preregistered *development-corpus decoder engineering assay*, not independent evidence of synthetic character identity. **Date:** 2026-10-10 (America/Chicago). **Lineage:** [E4-B order crossover](../results/eidolon/E4B_ORDER_CROSSOVER_RESULTS_V01.md): all 384/384 Noetic and 384/384 fixed-W predictions followed the final training label, independent of the held-out cue. E4-A/E4-B original nulls are retained unmodified.

## Hypothesis

A closed-form, balanced, **simultaneously fitted** linear readout should remove the specific *sequential last-teacher recency failure* in native motor supervision. A decoder that is order-insensitive is *not necessarily discriminative*. Independently test (1) order invariance, (2) training-set separability, (3) held-out top-1/context diversity, and (4) whether any gain is truly carried by learned recurrent W rather than the decoder.

## Frozen protocol

- Reuse original E4-A train/test corpus and E4-B seeds `151,167,179,191,211,223`, 128 native Pretorius neurons. Data were inspected in multiple prior experiments: this is an instrument-development comparison **not sealed confirmation**.
- E4-B Noetic W is learned via its unchanged 160 source-fixture teaching episodes. Same-seed virgin W and all other native input/encoding coordinates are identical. No authoritative Pretorius brain or event store is modified.
- For each input cue, reset native fast neural dynamics and emit an **unlabeled 128-d normalized native neural-rate feature** after a cue and after 3 and 12 input-free steps, with native `learn=False` at every feature-extraction tick. Only the **32 training examples** and their declared labels are used to solve the decoder. No held-out labels, actor names or episode identifiers are used to fit parameters. The same three feature time positions are used on virgin W and trained W (96 feature rows each; balanced 24 examples per target class).
- Target and output include **all ten existing Pretorius actions**, with four positive supervised action classes. Solve a deterministic centered multi-output ridge system with `alpha=0.10`, intercept as per-class mean target rate, and no sequential motor-gradient updates. The ridge readout lives only in a new research module; it does not replace or edit `motor_w`, `motor_b` or the existing neural `reinforce_action` function.
- Five declared conditions, each with 16 held-out cases per seed and three time positions (0/3/12): (A) same-seed virgin recurrent W + balanced decoder; (B) Noetic-trained W + balanced decoder; (C) trained Noetic W's decoder **with W replaced by virgin same-seed W** (no re-fitting); (D) Noetic W + existing trained *sequential motor decoder* (explicit historical failure reference); (E) same-seed virgin W with the independently **retrained virgin-W balanced decoder** (software equivalence control, counted once in scientific comparisons).
- Readout **order invariance**: hold neural weights and feature observations fixed, reorder their training rows through four predeclared rotations (by class order) and refit the entire ridge system. Scores must match within 1e-6 (finite-precision tolerance). This must not inadvertently rotate Noetic recurrent training itself; the hypothesis concerns *decoder training order*.
- Report all seed/condition/task/delay row-level action scores, training- and held-out top-1, mean target probabilities, exact predicted-class diversity, same-readout W-swap effects and per-seed baseline changes. A trained recurrent gain requires improved held-out choice over virgin-W ridge **and** substantial degradation when the trained W is replaced without altering the fitted decoder. If only ridge helps, that is a decoder effect; if every arm is constant-class, record the null. No parameters may be changed after seeing test results.
- Confirm shape/finite/probability/determinism contracts, equal feature/teacher budgets, identical same-seed W coordinate topology, virgin-W no update, and that the readout fitting has not mutated any native W/motor weights. No learn flags during inference, no source ownership claims, no production integration.

The main failure mode is *lack of separable native neural representations*. An order-invariant linear classifier can still fail on unseen actors and paraphrases, and even a positive score on this reused corpus does not prove semantic generalization or persistent identity. The next confirmatory gate still requires an independently sealed provenance-grounded task-outcome corpus and stronger direct text/lexical baselines.
