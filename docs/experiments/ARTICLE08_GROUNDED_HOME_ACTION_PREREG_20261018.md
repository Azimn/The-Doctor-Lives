# Pretorius home: grounded action arbitration, frozen remediation experiment

**Registered:** October 18, 2026, before implementing or running the new arbiter. **Dependency:** prior Article 8 PR #78's 21/24 `avoid` outputs, arbitrary avoid→tea mapping, and repeated denial on depleted tea.

## Problem and proposed fix

The neural substrate outputs generic tendencies, **not household verbs**. Treating `avoid` as "brew tea" produced nonsensical repetitions. **Replace the hard mapping**, not the neural weights or the simulated world's physics.

The new opt-in **engineer/host-owned** arbiter checks actual executable affordances using the pre-existing Laboratory's pure, replayable world transition for each complete activity (walk, light, and final act). It then ranks genuinely feasible household opportunities with the **unchanged Article 7 `motive_scores` function**, optionally incorporating the real Doctor's existing bounded neural score distribution. No fabricated first-person engineering numbers or invented object motions are shown to Pretorius. This is not yet a learned neural policy or free-roaming independent agent.

Allowed activities are the original, finite, source-backed subset: experimental work, repair, a meal, tea, tidying/cleaning, and rest. The arbiter refuses unsafe/unavailable actions and explains the refusal only to host diagnostics. It must not silently refill supplies or rewrite old world events. If no activity remains possible, **abstain** rather than hallucinate.

## Matched pre-run test

Three material conditions with genuine setup action receipts: ordinary morning; all three cups of tea actually consumed (real scarcity); and instruments damaged by two prior authentic experiments. An additional after-dark situation tests whether lamp use is genuinely feasible. Three original seeded neural instances `1842`, `2718`, `31415`, with **six candidate decisions per trial** under each case; each arm begins from matched source states and receives a verified first-person initial frame.

Arms:
- **legacy-mapped** (frozen ten-action→tea/tidy mapping from PR #78), with no replacement after denied actions;
- **grounded+Doctor**, existing real BrainPort cognition plus frozen motive scores and actual affordance simulation;
- **grounded simple rule**, same availability, same base motive scores but without neural bias.

Host performs the necessary common physical route and lamps for any approved task. Real user-visible first-person perceptions and durable world action source receipts are required. The decision can be retried only from unchanged world state; changes require a fresh choice. Trial pass/fail metrics include actual accepted/completed activities, denials, attempts on absent resources, task diversity, source ledger replay, corresponding Doctor memory admissions and total assistant-originating vs host-controlled action counts.

**Predeclared checks**: in the tea-exhausted world, neither grounded arm may attempt a missing tea action; every grounded execution must have been feasible in the pre-action pure transition; no invisible/out-of-stock activity may count as completed; all real physical worlds must replay. Grounded arms should outperform the legacy hard mapping on materially accepted actions after tea runs out, or the proposed correction failed. Do **not** claim the neural component adds value if the cheap grounded controller ties or wins; both scores must be displayed.

## Limitations and adjudication

Even successful results establish a **safe grounded host arbitration improvement**, not endogenous desire, subjective enjoyment, durable neural habit learning, independent evaluation or the ability to do anything in an open-world simulation. The actual Doctor is the single neural/identity/memory authority and sees only natural first-person world experiences; numeric home information remains in the trusted engineer executor. The Laboratory stays the **only** authority for objects, supplies, physics, clock, permissions and event receipts.

Keep **Article 8 at 10/100**, Article 7 at 80/100, all qualification gates Unassessable and the aggregate unchanged. This is a new remediation pilot stacked on unmerged Article 8 research; no production or lived canonical Doctor is altered.
