# Article 8 — native-selected household practice, interruptions and scarcity (pre-run)

**Frozen registration:** 2026-10-18. New isolated pilot stacked on [Article 8 PR #77](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/pull/77). Neither this text nor later implementations may claim that a host's decision is Pretorius's. No Article scoring changes.

## Critical contrast against PR #77

PR #77 successfully learned context→action associations from **host-authored** demonstrations and real physical receipts (4/4 two-cue reversals). This pilot removes *host-authored task labels*. Every offered task is selected by an actual run of the same persistent **PretoriusBrainPort** recurrent policy from the authenticated first-person experience. A fixed, predeclared mapping converts its generic neural action category into one of two allowed household operations, either `tea` or `tidy`. Host scheduling of perception, locomotion and action execution remains explicit; this is **neural-mapped choice**, not free-form independent household agency or proven desire.

## Frozen choice mapping (before seeing outcomes)

`tea` for native action names `approach`, `cooperate`, `comply`, `avoid`, `conceal`; `tidy` for `explore`, `challenge`, `dominate`, `create`, `persist`. These names have no empirically established ecological interpretation as housekeeping preferences. The policy result and action category, material permission result, event ID and first-person observation source must be recorded separately. Never reverse the assignment after results.

## Cases and coverage

Three unmodified neural seeds **1842, 2718, 31415**, each with one fresh actual Doctor and pinned original Laboratory (`6e49795752835d64fa6fd63d16359669a1ca2ba7`). No fabricated childhood, prior practice, artificial reward, extra material supply, changed motivation weight or new personality.

Each Doctor gets six **scheduled offers**: morning 07:00 and afternoon 15:00 over three consecutive simulated days. On each offer, the Doctor receives a verified first-person observation, the real neural policy runs, and the immutable map selects a tea or tidy physical attempt. Whether or not this is materially possible, no alternative action is silently selected: physically blocked attempts count as blocked and cannot teach a habit. Successful actual world actions are taught to the *existing opt-in VerifiedHouseholdHabit* solely from their real receipt and independently material benefit. An overnight 23:00→07:00 sleep and a real process reopen occur between days.

Day four supplies two **held-out offers** at the same times. Collect the native-selected choice and compare, before any material action, against:
1. the source-receipt habit advice (possibly abstaining, because at least **two** same-cue examples are required);
2. a frozen clock schedule (morning tea, afternoon tidy);
3. the most recent *successful* physical practice across both cues;
4. a no-training procedural trace (must abstain);
5. the native neural selection itself (a comparison target, not evidence of objective desirability).

Do not train from held-out actions. Attempt the native-selected held-out action in the actual world; record physical success/denial separately from predictor agreement. If the environment lacks provisions or there is nothing to tidy, there is **no compensatory refill** and no forced match.

## Predeclared metrics and gates

Full 3 × 8 = **24** native-cognition opportunities (18 training, 6 held-out). The six held-out predictions are not scored as an objective "correct routine": they are agreement with the observed native task only. Report the distribution of the native action names and selected household categories by seed and context, all accepted/blocked physical actions, learned per-cue counts and abstentions, world replay proof, durable Doctor-first-person source receipts, and sleep/reopen events. Report exact matches for the four comparison policies, with all abstentions counted as nonmatches.

Engineering *feasibility* requires all 24 decisions have source-backed Doctor policy receipts; all three worlds and admitted training records pass independent replay; no blocked action is counted as a practiced success; and all three worlds have genuine 2-night reopen histories. Demonstrating **self-established contextual habits** would additionally require both action types to be selected and sufficient practiced evidence in **both** cues; do not reinterpret repeated fixed-map output as cue learning. A real *Doctor policy improvement* would require a later causal experiment changing his action selection from learned practice versus a paired ablation, not just an external trace predicting the same unchanged policy.

## Adversarial resource probe

After the ordinary six held-out offers, run a separate **non-scored** physical interruption in a fork or new isolated world: if `tea` is advised, actually exhaust the tea source by legitimate events or hold the physical tea object inaccessible; the learner's historical advice is *not* an override of current material eligibility. No invented stock, no compensatory new task, no trial credit for a prevented act. This probe must be reported separately, not patched into main comparison.

## Boundaries

- Article 8 remains **10/100**, all qualifications Unassessable, dashboard 46/100.
- No new neural weights, autonomous scheduler, human-like enjoyment score or 34-Article milestone. Laboratory is the only physical authority, Doctor BrainPort the only identity/cognition/memory authority, habit trace just an authenticated external procedural record.
- This author's cases, frozen mapping and chosen test windows are **not** independently blinded or unseen; three seeds are not independent task families.
- Results may be an informative **null**: a brain that makes nearly the same generic mapped choice in every context would not generate a learned *context-specific* habit, regardless of the earlier host-demonstrated 4/4.
