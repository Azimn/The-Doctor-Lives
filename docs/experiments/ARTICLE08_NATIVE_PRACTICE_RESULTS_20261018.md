# Article 8 native-selected household practice — executed null adjudication

**Experiment:** 2026-10-18. **PR:** stacked on Article 8 PR #77. **Registration:** [ARTICLE08_NATIVE_PRACTICE_PREREG_20261018.md](ARTICLE08_NATIVE_PRACTICE_PREREG_20261018.md) was committed before the experiment code or first CI run. **Lab pin:** `6e49795752835d64fa6fd63d16359669a1ca2ba7`.

**Final empirical ruling: no verified endogenous context-specific habit advantage.** The 4/4 result in PR #77's host-taught routine experiment does **not** transfer to the Doctor's actually selected generic neural actions under this frozen two-action mapping. Keep **Article 8 at 10/100**, qualification **Unassessable**, independent transfer and causal gate unchanged, dashboard unchanged.

## What happened physically

Real Doctor BrainPort instances with independent seeds **1842, 2718, 31415** each encountered morning and afternoon in their original persistent Laboratory over three training days, slept and reopened every night, then underwent two day-four holdout comparisons. The study contains **24 authenticated first-person/neural-choice opportunities**: 18 training and six held-out. The host still initiated all opportunities and scheduled movement, clock and sleep. **Only the two-action category proposal** came from actual neural-policy output through a *frozen, arbitrary* mapping. No free-roaming or true internal habitual intention is established.

The **6/6** held-out predictions by recency *do not prove recency is an intelligent household policy*: they merely matched an almost constant native selection in this particular study. The habit trace also abstained correctly rather than manufacturing a learned habit from insufficient practice. The six material denials are important real-world evidence: a generic neural `avoid` label, arbitrarily mapped to `tea`, cannot justify drinking nonexistent provisions. The environment did not replenish tea or silently choose an alternate task.

## Engineering outcome

- The opt-in `VerifiedHouseholdHabit` never learns from a blocked action or from tea that yields no material benefit.
- Actual Doctor first-person input and neural selection are recorded together, without exposed raw engineering internals; the Doctor still owns his sole identity and memory.
- Physical world consequences, original Laboratory source receipts, procedural practice trace and three restart cycles are independently verified.
- There is no reverse-engineered context preference, intentional attraction to tea, subjective enjoyment, spontaneous task initiation, or proof that repeated action lowered the Doctor's decision cost.
- The study **does not** satisfy the stronger Article 8 criteria. Nothing in this record warrants even the 25-point mechanism gate for *Doctor-originated* habits, although it confirms a useful experimental harness and the external engineered procedural-learning component from PR #77.

### Next engineering hypothesis (a separate *future*, independently registered test)

Replace this arbitrary mapping between ten generic neural labels and two household acts with a **real environmentally grounded affordance selection policy**. The Doctor should be offered only what physically exists and can be used, through verified natural-language perceptions. Compare a simple state-aware game controller and a fixed n-gram procedure against learned alternatives. Introduce a true alternative to exhausted tea—rest, workbench preparation, reading or cleaning—without silently substituting actions. Register independent held-out context switches and demonstrate better *materially valuable choices* against cheap controls, not mere agreement with a static neural label.

The Laboratory is a home: activities may be enjoyable rather than driven by shortages. Do not mistake six denials for desired hardship or treat scarce tea as a design victory.
