# Article 7 — Motivation: preregistered single-variable home interventions

**Pre-run registration:** 2026-10-16.  
**Intended engineering gate:** `causal_controls` (20 points) **only if** the accepted source-verified intervention produces discriminating evidence in the real experimental Doctor and its bodily-priority ablation. This is NOT a proposal for a 100/100 Article score, an Article qualification, or independent held-out transfer.

## Previous evidence and why it did not settle the gate

The earlier four-scenario pilot reported native neural action tendency
**1/4**, neural+household motives **3/4**, bodily-motive lesion **2/4**, and
literal text rules **4/4**. However the supposed *broken-apparatus*
intervention also accumulated fatigue through the setup experiments.
Thus resting rather than repairing could be reasonable, and the old
trial did **not cleanly isolate apparatus damage**. We explicitly
preserve that result without adjusting the published motive weights.

## Locked intervention plan (before CI execution)

This new pilot uses five verified *engineered counterfactual starting
worlds*. They are synthetic research fixtures, **not simulated prior
autobiographical experiences**. The `DailyRhythm` genesis has an explicit
`engineer_counterfactual_seed_NOT_lived_history` provenance marker; no
physical acts, prior training, or invented memories are attributed to
Pretorius before his first observed current-state experience.

| Arm setting | Sole engineered differences from the baseline | Expected physical goal |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline | None | Perform scientific experiment |
| Wear only | Apparatus health 2 → 0 | Repair apparatus |
| Fatigue only | Fatigue 2 → 8 | Rest in bedroom |
| Thirst only | Thirst 2 → 8 | Make and drink tea |
| Combined, longitudinal | Apparatus 2 → 0 **and** fatigue 2 → 8 | First rest, then return and repair |

All other initial resource counts, clocks, light sources, station, clothes,
physical equipment, source identities and conditions are held constant
within these engineered setups. Each condition is run across **five
controllers**:

1. Actual 128-neuron Doctor native policy and frozen ten-action task mapping.
2. Actual Doctor native policy **plus** the unchanged simple environmental
   motive-priority layer from PR #72.
3. Same physical affordance/selection layer **with bodily motive pressure
   disabled** (but physical impossibilities still enforced).
4. The existing cheap **literal first-person** text-rule controller.
5. A newly frozen independent **numeric-state simple rule**, given the
   **same hidden engineering-side body state available to the motive
   arbitration layer**. It selects rest for fatigue ≥ 7, tea for thirst ≥ 8,
   repair for broken apparatus, and experiment otherwise, in that order.

The subject receives *only* verified natural-language first-person
experience through the original Doctor BrainPort bridge in every arm.
The recurrent policy executes in every arm for parity of contact with the
actual Doctor, but external controllers may not use its recommendation.
The numeric-rule arm is essential to evaluating whether the more complex
ecological controller adds anything beyond a cheap equally informed rule.

**Fairness:** Every arm gets the same first first-person observation,
verified by hash, and identical modeled initial physical conditions within
each scenario; a maximum of **12 world actions total**. The same host
provides routing between adjacent Laboratory stations and a permitted
lamp when needed, but host navigation is NOT attributed to autonomous
Doctor planning. Any action success is recorded only when independent
physical state and event receipts verify it. Full before/after states
and numerical priorities remain researcher-side.

The dual-need case permits **two episodes**: finish the first task, then
observe the changed world, let the actual policy run again, and attempt
the second task. If episode one does not physically resolve the first
need, no second episode is credited. This is a modest form of persistent
activity resumption, **not a prewritten verbal script** or evidence of
an independent desire to repair machinery. The expected sequence and
thresholds are locked here and in code.

## Registered causal prediction

Changing *only* the physical need should change the external motive
controller's executable action. Its body-priority lesion should be
meaningfully worse on at least one controlled condition, with the
Laboratory independently refusing impossible actions. The numerical
simple rule might match or beat the motive controller; report ties as
ties. The combined condition should produce actual rest, reduction in
fatigue and then physical apparatus repair, with both action stages
committed to the original world event history.

To justify a **20-point causal-controls gate** for the **engineering
mechanism** of Article 7, demand: all paired states/observations match;
all source receipts verify; at least one material divergence between
motive regulation and its lesion; real single-variable contrasts across
wear/fatigue/thirst; a demonstrated longitudinal combined-need
interruption/recovery; no fabricated first-person memory. If those pass,
only that engineering gate is eligible. A stronger Article qualification
must still use independent held-out transfer with separately registered
assessment criteria. Do not count repeated test invocations as
independent conditions.

## Known limits

The motive controller is an **external deterministic ecological
mechanism**, not an emergent conscious drive or learned native neural
motivation. The treatment receives numeric engineering-side state;
the unmodified neural/first-person-rule arms do not, hence the fifth
equal-information numeric controller. Tests use **five authored
conditions, one small 128-neuron brain per arm** and fixed native
weights, without independent unseen schedules or a blinded evaluator.
A successful report does not establish general agency, true hunger
experience, a full day of autonomous free play, or Article 14 decision
rationale quality.

The operation is standalone inside the Laboratory. The public
Frankenstein Village and Pretorius's period film autobiographical
history remain untouched.
