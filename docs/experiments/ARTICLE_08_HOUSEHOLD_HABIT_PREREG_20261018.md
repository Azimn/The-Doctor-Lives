# Article 8: household habit acquisition — frozen engineering screen

**Pre-execution registration: 2026-10-18.** Experimental, opt-in, isolated branch only. **Article 8 remains 10/100**; no Article qualification is asserted.

## Test purpose

Can a bounded, persisted, explicitly **host-owned procedural trace** acquire different cue→action associations from actual source-replayed household activity, retain them through real time, sleep and process restart, and reverse them when independently verified experiences reverse? The learner is not the Doctor's consciousness or neuron-level habit plasticity. The Doctor's existing single BrainPort must receive a verified first-person observation and undergo cognition per learning opportunity; host-directed physical actions remain correctly attributed to the host in the study.

## Predeclared domain

Kitchen, two naturally recurring temporal contexts (07:00 morning and 15:00 afternoon), two ordinary tasks, **tea** (brew_tea) and **tidy** (clean). These are fully supported by the pinned existing world physics and do not require a new kitchen, another inventory or social agent.

Two separate, independent living-world sequences, each physically replayed:
- **Mapping A**: morning tea; afternoon tidy.
- **Mapping B**: morning tidy; afternoon tea.

Each association receives exactly **two** world-verified successful examples, over two simulated days, before the third day held-out retrieval. No artificial reinforcement, hand-supplied performance score, synthetic prior memory, language-model-minted outcomes or pretrained association. If world conditions prevent any action, the trial must record failure rather than reset hidden stocks or replay training differently. A full overnight sleep and clock progression are genuine persisted physical events; no spontaneous activity during the gap is claimed.

All words experienced by Doctor flow solely through the existing authenticated first-person DoctorHomeBridge. Brain is reopened from the same saved storage at the interruption. An individual observation is never a raw engineering JSON prompt.

## Frozen controls

1. Newly acquired *cue-conditioned habit* from durable authenticated action receipts only. Advisory, not an autonomous executor. One count for each verified material success; at least two same-cue training receipts needed to advise.
2. **Fixed clock schedule**: morning tea, afternoon tidy. No adaptation.
3. **Simple recency**: most recent physically performed training task across all cues.
4. **Unchanged Sims-style bodily motive**: choose higher eligible tea or clean utility from the existing `motive_scores`; same numeric host state as habit feature extractor, no privileged new source.
5. **No-training lesion**: same habit module with empty store must abstain.

Report correctness **for both reversed mappings**, with exactly matched observation/test day circumstances within each mapping. All baselines are scored against the identical material world before the habit executes; no alternate physical state may improve a baseline.

## Registered success/failure criteria

- Total four held-out decisions (two contexts × two mappings); habit must choose **4/4**, fixed schedule **2/4** at most, recency **0/4** in alternating morning/afternoon presentation (or record any deviation), and no-training must abstain **4/4**. Frozen motive rule is descriptive; it may match or exceed habit.
- A real post-training restart, overnight world time progression, first-person Doctor continuity, replay-verified physical activity, no cross-room or cross-world receipt promotion.
- Genuine physical continuation after recall: execute each advised action in its correct source world and verify the material outcome, if environmental resources permit; distinguish choice accuracy from act completion.
- After a counterexample in the same cue, old learning must **not be rewritten** or forged; increment exact verified evidence and report tie/uncertainty rather than claiming a new stable habit after one exception.
- Reject fabricated event ids/hashes, altered before snapshots, duplicate receipts, source conflicts and reward spoofing.
- No score promotion, regardless of results. This is engineered procedural learning after **host-selected demonstrations**, not genuine endogenous Doctor action selection or randomized independently blinded human-likeness evaluation.
