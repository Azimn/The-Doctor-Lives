# Article 8: reversible household habits — executed engineering screen

**Date:** 2026-10-18. **Status:** experimental source-verified demonstration only; **Article 8 remains 10/100**.

## Predeclared conditions and outcomes

The preregistration [ARTICLE_08_HOUSEHOLD_HABIT_PREREG_20261018.md](ARTICLE_08_HOUSEHOLD_HABIT_PREREG_20261018.md) was committed on a separate earlier commit before the new mechanism/runner. The intervention uses two independent source-verified real Laboratory world timelines (mapped oppositely), two ordinary dayparts in the kitchen, and two material activities: preparing/drinking tea or cleaning the kitchen. A real PretoriusBrainPort receives authenticated first-person perceptions and cognition is invoked, but the **host demonstrator explicitly selects all training actions**. No independent preference or autonomous habit formation by Pretorius has been demonstrated.

Each actual world acquired two **authenticated physically successful examples per cue** over two simulated days, physically slept overnight, reopened both world and real Doctor brain, then encountered the same cue on day three. The stored trace can make **advisory recommendations only**, and they were actually executed and independently verified in the real Laboratory.

### Final held-out decisions (across both reversed mappings)

| Policy | Registered cue/action pairs correctly chosen /4 |
| --- | ---: |
| Source-verified procedural practice trace (2 completed examples needed) | **4/4** |
| Original fixed morning-tea/afternoon-tidy clock schedule | **2/4** |
| One-action recency | **0/4** |
| Original bounded `motive_scores` restricted to the domain's tea/clean alternatives | **3/4** |
| No-training trace lesion | **0/4** |

All four habit-advised actions produced **real material consequences**, and all original household action histories and learned receipt sources independently replay. The preregistered engineering screen passed.

**Important fairness limitation:** `motive_scores` is frozen and unchanged from an earlier Laboratory motive study; its `clean` affordance is calculated from the sanctum work area, whereas this task occurs in the kitchen. The numeric result is reported descriptively as an unchanged off-domain controller, **not a strong kitchen-calibrated competitor**. A future fair comparison should include an equal-information kitchen-specific procedural script and a clock-independent dynamic simple rule.

**Temporal limitation:** The two training passes and third-day check are *scheduled host demonstrations/observations*, not unprompted activity over days. There is no subjective evidence of a pleasurable or personally chosen routine. The same training data persist only as external procedural source records; they are not installed as the Doctor's autobiographical identity or neural weights.

## Authenticity and restart checks

No text label or numeric reward can be passed to the learner to simulate success. A practice event is admitted only after an actual Laboratory event with the allowed op, matching before/after digests, a genuinely helpful physical consequence, and an exact source-admitted first-person Doctor memory. Merely supplying a world hash for an unrelated action is insufficient.

Before every advice operation, the code replays historical Laboratory events and recomputes the authentic **time-of-day cue** and **physical material gain**, checking against the stored trace. Fabricating a receipt, relabeling an authentic morning source as an afternoon one, altering an event hash, duplicating a source, or using a stale/currently false body pose or world snapshot fails before a learned recommendation.

A fresh empty control trace offers no habit because the minimum two practiced actions per context have not happened. After a fully supported restart, exactly four training events remain valid in each of the two independent source worlds.

## Adjudication: research only

Article 8 **remains 10/100**, with no new tracker gate, no full-domain Article qualification and no change to the 46/100 aggregate. This pilot establishes that a cheap and source-bound procedural mechanism *can* persist cue-dependent household patterns and override a static schedule across reversed examples under real physical conditions. It **does not** establish that Pretorius himself spontaneously acquires automatic habits, saves deliberation effort through practice, values the routine, or generalizes to unseen contexts.

### Next discriminating gate

Replace host-selected demonstrations with **doctor-selected** but materially verifiable choices and independently generated **novel household cue families**. Preregister a controller that uses exactly the same action history as the new procedural trace but applies one-step recency, a two-step n-gram, and a fully kitchen-aware clock rule, plus resource interruption cases that physically disable the learned act. For the habit claim, measure repetition-specific decline in real decision effort or action cost, not simply matching an alternating author-taught association. Train and evaluate under different material conditions; preserve negative evidence and no automatic score movement.

This is a step toward Pretorius developing his own domestic preferences in a consistent home, not a proof that he already has them.
