## Interpretation and stopping rule

The novel behavior is durable linkage from a Doctor intention to a
world-observed prerequisite, bounded action and evidence-based completion.
The current dispatcher contains no learned planning mechanism. The
simple baseline ties when given the same local observation. There is no
held-out distribution, independent evaluator, longitudinal deployment,
renderer substitution result, unprompted scheduler service, or claim of
Article-level support.

Article 17 goal structure, 18 prospection, 19 agency, 4 memory, and 22
internal causality now have narrower engineering probes available.
No full-domain Article changes from Unassessable on this evidence alone.

Next discriminating tasks need delayed conditions; unexpected world
changes; unrelated task interference; source-memory lesion and replay;
cross-book, cross-room and cross-tool tasks; and a blinded simple
controller with equivalent information, permissions, and time budget.
Later tests should introduce competing commitments with shared
budgets, reliable interruption recovery and outcomes that change
future choices.
