## Causal interpretation and attribution

The demonstrated intervention is **whether an actual source-verified read appears in the original active autobiographical store**, and the measured downstream effect is a **host-owned bounded manual interpreter's physical work proposal**. The result is stronger than a change in outward book preference because the verified proposal can drive an actual repair through the authoritative Laboratory, with a separately hashed action receipt.

However **the correct mapping is not generated by Noesis**. A deterministic two-action grammar extracts the ordered instruction from the real page, and a frozen default proposes experiment when that instruction is missing or inapplicable. The real Doctor neural policy was not shown to form a novel procedural rule; the host offers the work opportunity, selects the granular action and separately authorizes it. The Laboratory provides physics and adjudicates success. The fact that a one-line rule with the same information would also select repair is an **expected tie**, not evidence of superiority.

The candidate has no general natural-language instruction following, unknown-tool transfer or genuinely self-composed plan. Book text is not motor authority; only source-checked content may contribute to the narrowly defined proposal.

## Failure and scope register

No negative outcomes were observed in the four authored tests. There is **one primary authored worn-apparatus condition**, one book variation, one unworn condition, and no independent blinded holdout or multiple neural seeds. The fixed 128-neuron configuration is inherited from previous research but not causally tested for this host interpreter. Do not interpret a 4/4 unit-test result as 4 independent human-like learning demonstrations.

**No Article promotion:** Article 15 learning, Article 19 agency, D3 through D11, and V432.6B remain under their existing gates. No new neural weights, independent knowledge store, first-person content route or production default was changed.
