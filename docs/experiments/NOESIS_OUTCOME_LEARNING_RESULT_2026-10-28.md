## Claim boundaries

This is **causally effective autobiographical consequence sensitivity on one authored real work scenario**. The valuation uses exact, researcher-specified first-person phrase patterns and weights. It is not an agent-discovered causal law, general reinforcement learning, open-vocabulary action grounding, or a demonstrated behavioral advantage over an equal-information deterministic controller. Native recurrent score contribution was observed, but the final choice's neural necessity across seeds was not established. The host still supplies an opportunity and independently authorizes motor actions. No broad autonomous cycle, blind holdout, 4,096-neuron full-size longitudinal sample, end-to-end global first-person no-bypass, six-organ graduation or product release was tested here.
