## What this does NOT prove

The two-action mapping and natural-language grammar are researcher-defined. An instruction can change Noesis's selected action, but Noesis is not independently deriving a general causal strategy from trial outcomes. Its ten native action tendencies influence candidate probabilities, but the evidence bonus can dominate that influence. The no-learning comparison is strongest where the neural prior is separately ablated, and does not prove an all-seed action flip under neural priors enabled.

A simple rule given the same manual and observation also chooses repair; no superiority is claimed. The host supplies opportunities and legal offers, not independent initiative. Novel books with the same authored grammar are **variation, not blinded held-out transfer**. There is no model-renderer test on this choice head or long-term self-regulated skill learning.

**Existing six-organ D3-D11, V432.6B and Article 15 gates remain unqualified.** Preserve exact negative results and original baseline.
