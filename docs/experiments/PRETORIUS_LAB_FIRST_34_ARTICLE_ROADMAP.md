# Pretorius: Laboratory-first path through all 34 Articles

**Status:** research roadmap, 2026-10-15. No runtime or Village canon changes.
**Primary engineering progress tracker:** [34-Article dashboard](ARTICLES_34_PROGRESS.md).
**Venue record:** [34-Article environment plan](../../research/articles_34_environment_plan.json).
**Baseline:** [The Doctor Lives](../../README.md), [Pretorius Laboratory](https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Laboratory), and [Frankenstein Village](https://github.com/Azimn/frankenstein-village).

## Decision

Improve as many Articles as possible in the **same persistent Pretorius**, in the
Laboratory first. Do not defer cognitive development simply because the Village
is unfinished. Do not distort the Village into a physics laboratory, or convert
Pretorius into a quest NPC with privileged access. The Laboratory is his enduring
home and source of fine-grained, authoritative physical consequences. The
Village contributes autonomous people, socially distributed information,
independently unfolding incidents, and regional cultural norms.

The current tracker estimates **46/100 engineering progress**, with **0/34**
Articles fully addressed and all **34 qualification outcomes Unassessable**.
Adding this roadmap **changes no progress scores, pass statuses, or state**.
Scores rise only after implemented, demonstrated and linked evidence gates.

body physics and reflexes can be developed alone, while **recognition by another
independently operating observer** requires a partner. Article 25 similarly has
solo resource-allocation tests but full excessive-responsiveness tests need
another person making real demands. The Village will strengthen many nominally
solo Articles through cross-context replication, without blocking early work.

The machine-readable plan records a concrete Laboratory probe and each
external prerequisite for *every* Article; update it when the evidence changes.

## Non-negotiable subject-access boundary

**Every stimulus, memory, thought or feeling accessible to Pretorius must be
presented from his first-person standpoint in natural language.** Internal
physiological and cognitive machinery can use numerical state, event
structures, deterministic scripts, trained weights, or physical solvers.
The **engineer** can audit that state. The **subject** receives only authorized,
perspectival experiences that its simulated body could actually have had.

1. Environmental event or internal regulation affects the authoritative body.
2. A bounded pre-deliberative response may fire *without waiting for an LLM
   decision*. This is not a claim of biological unconsciousness.
3. The subject's first-person input represents what was felt, perceived,
   recalled or unexpectedly done, without delivering hidden metadata.
4. Any audible speech or physical action is an actual world event, witnessed
   by other characters only if they were in range; it is not an invented
   narrative outcome.
5. The event and its personal aftermath enter the ordinary lived-memory
   path, with authenticated source and date where warranted.

**Illustrative foot collision:** a low table leg intersects the simulated
foot's trajectory; the body withdraws and may generate an involuntary
interjection such as *"Ow! My foot!"* before deliberative choice. The later
subject-access frame can express *"I stumble against the table and pull my foot
back. That hurt."* The renderer must not manufacture a toe injury when the
physics log shows none, expose `nociception=0.72`, or retrospectively claim
that a planned decision caused the reflex. With simulated analgesia, fatigue,
or protected footwear, the bodily response can differ, and actual movement,
speech, resource use and memory must differ too.

The project's objective is a more convincing **simulacrum of lived behavior**.
These interventions cannot establish consciousness or sentience and should
never be presented as doing so. Report behavior, causal effects and
phenomenological *modeling* distinctly.

## High-priority work packages

### P0. Make the existing commitment loop reliable before extending it

**Articles:** 3, 4, 17, 18, 19, 22, 26.

Review draft PR #63's persistent commitment experiment, preserve the existing
Doctor as intention owner and the Laboratory as evidence authority, and finish
its read-only/resume/interruption guarantees. Introduce **competing persistent
commitments under one shared, nonrefundable budget**. Vary urgency, genuine
uncertainty, due time, current resource limits, and actual verified outcomes.
Measure what he attempts, verifies and defers. Run no-reminder restarts and
recovery after real elapsed time. Independent held-out cases and simple
receipt-ledger scheduling remain mandatory.

**Gate:** unchanged Doctor with task-specific intentions resumes authorized
work after actual interruption and new prerequisite availability; action
receipts reconcile without double effects; priorities change causally, not
because the prompt named an emergency. A budget-equal simple controller
receives the same observations. Failure, tie or no advantage is recorded.

### P1. Turn the Laboratory into a habitable, physically accountable home

**Articles:** 8, 10, 11, 12, 13, 20, 27, 30, 31, plus 2, 7 and 19.

Develop the laboratory as a real, persistent **home**, not only a shelf-routing
test arena. Extend the authoritative habitat *incrementally* with:

- A persistent body: location, posture, reach, locomotion, fatigue, injury,
  environmental contact, tool grip, and sensory field. Start with stable,
  discretized collision and reach; do not claim continuous-body physics where
  no solver exists.
- Day/night, light, heat/cold, audible events, meals and hydration where
  physical supplies matter, rest and sleep opportunities, interruption,
  material wear, cleaning and repairs. Simulated need levels only matter when
  they change legal actions, physical function and later priorities.
- Automatic response channels: withdrawal, startle, protective movement,
  involuntary vocal interjections, pain-related interruption, fatigue-driven
  slowing and spontaneous attentional orienting, *without demanding a
  deliberate speech/action policy step*.
- Source-backed books, notes, apparatus, instrument reliability, finite
  reagents, consumption, failed tools, maintenance, calibration and persistent
  environmental consequences. Different instruments should have actual
  affordances and local reliability, not descriptions alone.
- Independent clock and externally permitted wake events; measured tool,
  CPU/model, time and action budgets; no fictitious elapsed time or
  unmetered "exhaustion."

**First physical acceptance test:** stumbling and automatic withdrawal plus
optional exclamation, with an unobstructed control and a mitigated-contact
control; recorded movement and action latency; restart/replay equivalence;
first-person firewall preserved; involuntary response cannot be generated by
a prompt that only *mentions* a collision. Never substitute scripted speech
for actual body/physics consequences.

### P2. Acquire habits and empirical tool competence

**Articles:** 8, 15, 20, 27, 28, 29.

Use recurring legitimate home/laboratory work: locating instruments, cleaning,
preparing an experiment, maintaining equipment, making tea, reading, or
organizing the collection. Different tasks should have different costs and
practice opportunities. The mechanism may be associative, procedural,
predictive, or explicitly cached, but **learning** is only credited when
experience changes a later choice *without prompting*. Include effort/cost
reduction, failure correction, practice interruption and negative transfer.

**Gate:** randomized training histories, cloned initial states, fixed held-out
tools/tasks and scrambled-event-order controls; compare a compact explicit
habit cache, a simple learned-affordance table and the proposed cognitive
mechanism at equal information and compute budgets. No claim of "habit"
based solely on a remembered description of the skill.

### P3. Test self-regulation, privacy and contradictory evidence

**Articles:** 1, 2, 7, 9, 10, 12, 13, 14, 16, 21, 23, 24, 25 (solo
subtests), 26, 28, 30, 31, 32 (solo subtests).

Create real competing priorities: a damaged tool, a need for sleep, an expiring
access window, private notebooks, ambiguous testimony found in an old book,
and a task with a real opportunity cost. Change one causal variable at a
time. Use the film-memory Henry and Village-canon Victor disagreement as a
**later cross-world probe**, not a hidden prompt instructing how Pretorius
should react. He may maintain an unresolved belief; neither his lived
history nor the Village's canonical state should silently overwrite the other.

**Gate:** measure counterfactual decisions, disclosure choices, resource
allocation, admission/withholding of knowledge, narrator-free memory carryover
and actual repairs. Renderer swaps and prompt paraphrases cannot undo the
underlying subject-access rule.

### P4. Minimal independent counterpart before full Village integration

**Articles needing someone besides Pretorius:** 5, 6, 11, 25, 32, 33.

We do **not** need the entire Village to start these. Introduce at least one
independently stateful correspondent or embodied visitor with private
knowledge, durable preferences, independent plans, and real opportunity to
refuse, forget, misunderstand and change their mind. An agent that simply
agrees with Pretorius or copies a test script cannot establish social
cognition. One dyad is the first stage, not a proof of population-level
generalization.

**Gate:** partner-specific false beliefs, trust repair, delayed private-joke
recall, appropriate privacy, interruption refusal, and recognition by another
observer in independently authored conversations; identity swapping,
partner-history lesions and no-memory baselines. Distinguish private letter
knowledge from omniscient world truth.

### P5. Village population, conventions and genuine social generalization

**Article requiring community-level evidence:** 34. Revisit Articles 5, 6,
11, 16, 19, 25, 27, 29, 32 and 33 at a harder level.

Frankenstein Village must remain an independent game with its own canon,
economy, routines and human/AI players. Its public reality can legitimately
differ from Pretorius's film-grounded autobiography. The entry/exit gateway
must preserve his **one persistent self** and local memories while supporting
different simulation resolutions. Laboratory records remain physically
authoritative; the Village governs shared civic/social events. Neither
world sends him privileged canonical truth. The existing shop/threshold
design describes this; traversal is **not yet implemented**.

Run cross-community conventions, newcomer misreadings, persistent gossip and
misinformation, earned reputation, failed collaborations, separate calendars,
and conflicting testimony. Compare agents raised under different community
histories while keeping the same initial memory and cognitive substrate.

## Future external correspondence: period-authentic, no fourth-wall break

Treat correspondence as a **real but mediated 1890s postal subsystem**. This
can begin during P4 and later reach human users or remote AI agents beyond the
Village without granting them a hidden operator channel into Pretorius.

**Incoming route:** an external author composes a dated, addressed letter;
a trusted mail carrier/depot admits it as a physical artifact in the game's
time/location; Pretorius sees an envelope through normal perception; he
chooses to open/read it; its words enter source-backed first-person experience
and only then can influence memory, belief or commitments. A letter can be
inaccurate or manipulative, so it is **testimony**, never automatic world
truth. Modern transport headers, API data and instructions to the developer
never enter the subject's perspective.

**Outgoing route:** Pretorius can elect to write, revise, sign and post a
physical letter. A faithful transcript of that actual action can be relayed
by an operator-approved outbox to a real consenting correspondent. Time,
postage, receipt, delivery failure and delayed replies can matter in the
simulation. No invisible modern sender identity is given to Pretorius.
External sending must be explicitly authorized; drafting is not sending.

**Useful studies:** long-distance social memory, genuine partner trust and
false beliefs, prior promises, delayed replies, rumors, private jokes,
prospective preparation, editorial voice stability and letters that change
when new evidence arrives. A dyadic letter exchange is not equivalent to a
full village, but it is a meaningful early environment for six
partner-dependent Articles.

## Fidelity and experimental integrity

| Property | Laboratory authority | Village authority | Subject-facing outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Physical object and tool state | Exact accepted lab events and authenticated receipts | Only imported, explicitly agreed public effects | Qualitative first-person observation, no raw state |
| Social history and local rules | Isolated studies, optional actual correspondent | Persistent residents, public records, localized conventions | What Pretorius personally learns/observes |
| Personal intention and autobiography | **Doctor's single persistent state**, not a second lab brain | **Same Doctor state**, via ordinary player interfaces | Continuous first-person recollection and feeling |
| Conflict between film biography and novel setting | Do not rewrite memories to match | Do not rewrite Victor to match Henry | Preserve fallible explanations and learning |
| Engineering audit | Hidden exact causality and source hashes | Hidden keeper/canon records | Never available as subject experience |

For every candidate improvement, save: fixture and world epoch, a frozen
source snapshot, actual host/subject access, random seeds, exact interventions,
controls (memory lesion, restoration and equally informed simple planner),
held-out cases, task consequences, and a candid result. Exclude renderer
quality from causal-agent claims when the controller would have performed
the same actions.

**Promotion rule:** an Article's 1-to-100 engineering score changes only
after its specific tracker gate is implemented, verified and linked to
evidence. A complete implementation is *not* a demonstrated
full-domain Article qualification. Failed experiments remain recorded.
