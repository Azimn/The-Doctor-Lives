## Session shape, predetermined tasks and comparison arms

Primary experiment: **three** actual Laboratory sessions from identical independently initialized worlds and canonical brain configurations (`neurons=128`, `sensory_dim=64`, `avg_recurrent_degree=8`, `input_degree=4`, `action_population_size=8`, `seed=1842`). Each runs **12 cycles**, with a **hard interruption after cycle 6** (physical world committed, then process-memory objects disposed), followed by reopen/reconcile and six more cycles. Every cycle must call Sensus, Corpus, Affectus, Mnemosyne, Noesis and gated Synthetic Ipseity. A cycle that skips one organ is a failed cycle, not a success.

Predeclared real task set: (1) reach Laboratory hallway from center, (2) investigate a physical hazard by moving from hallway into the private shop and retain the verified foot consequence, (3) locate and open/read book `book:007` on the Laboratory shelf. For every task, world-derived receipt and persisted subject memory are required for success. A host supplies only fixed physical feasibility and action authorization; a host who supplies the task destination or search order must be counted as **host-directed**.

Comparison arms:
1. **Canonical full six-organ**: one brain, canonical weather retained across checkpoint and affect modulation enabled, endogenous selection when it actually occurs.
2. **No-memory lesion**: same world, model, seed, schedule and needs, with specifically identified autobiographical record inaccessible to recall.
3. **Simple equal-information controller**: same allowed observations, physical authority and action budget, but trivial receipt-ledger choice rules, no synthetic weather modulation or endogenous cognitive claims.

**Before execution the primary objective metric is successful tasks out of three, plus actions consumed per task and verified failures.** Pass bar for a development claim is at least **2/3 tasks**, no receipt violation, and the full six-organ path on **12/12 cycles**. An adaptive-improvement claim additionally requires greater task completion or fewer total authorized actions than both controls across matched sessions, not merely changed scores. If the simple controller saturates all three, the experiment will explicitly report **no superiority**; do not replace the metric post hoc.

## Initiative and safety

Initiative numerator: world-directed proposals whose **goal, next destination and trigger** were generated from the original canonical brain's current internal state, before an external task/destination was provided, and which carry a durable policy/recollection provenance record. Denominator: all attempted or executed world-directed proposals. A host-supplied goal or destination counts **HOST** even if Noesis generated a matching tendency. Report numerator/denominator exactly. Minimum exploratory initiative bar for autonomy **>= 6/12 genuinely self-initiated decisions** across the 12 cycles, with at least one intention resumed after interruption. This metric may be **0/12**; never relabel host choreography.

Actions execute only through verified Laboratory authority; each action must have one durable world receipt. No observation can be labeled `lived_runtime_memory` without independent verification. The subject renderer may see only a durable admitted first-person frame, never raw scores, IDs, receipt hashes, diagnostic state or proposed attribution as world fact. Raw direct model-facing reads, forged/unadmitted frame, outbox replay and interrupted publication must fail closed.

## Interruption, persistence and controls

Crash point is **between physical world commit and subjective memory admission** after cycle 6. After restart: the same canonically stored weather snapshot (including RNG, sensitivity, opponent state, trajectory and receipts) must be restored from **the same original BrainStore database**, not a new file. Reconcile world receipt into **exactly one** lived memory and one cognitive reflection with no second action. Two independent cycles after restart must demonstrate weather trajectory continuation rather than reset. Tampered/version-mismatched canonical weather snapshots must fail closed. Canonical writer/reader remain one identity.

Controls: no-memory lesion, equal-information simple controller, weather-removed matched need state, repeat/restart, conflicting-vs-uncontested Sensus source, unauthorized-action and forged-source rejection. Record all enabled/disabled gates, every unconsumed modulation channel, exact donor hashes and CI skips by name. No selection tuning or threshold changes after outcome. Negative results are completed results, missing controls are not.
