# Preview episodic-access repair: evidence-backed first-person continuity

**Date:** 2026-10-13
**Status:** Isolated draft PR #59; experimental, no production or Sensus donor changes
**Baseline run:** [38344525994](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/actions/runs/38344525994)
**Repaired-run comparison:** [38351434259](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/actions/runs/38351434259)
**Implementation:** `doctor_lives/three_organ_preview.py`, private preview host only.

## Failure being repaired

In the original five-scene trial, real Doctor cognition registered two conflicting observed balance readings (451 grams vs 919 grams), selected endogenous recall, and retained an earlier legitimate 325-gram memory. Later two-channel readings both said 451 grams. The Doctor admitted those observations and resolved the epistemic concern. Nevertheless, its generic salience-ranked `render_request` left the verified outcome out of the current and post-restart first-person renderer frames. This was a mismatch between the Doctor's durable evidence and the language organ's access, not a language-fluency failure.

## Implemented mechanism, within existing organs

The isolated three-organ preview now selects bounded verified episodes from the **Doctor's existing concern lifecycle and observed-event store**. No additional organ, owner, raw model prompt, new identity layer, or generalized production path was added.

An episode qualifies for first-person projection only when all these restrictions hold:

1. The Doctor concern is resolved, has the exact trusted conflict source and actor, and still has both its original opening event and an authenticated resolution event. The event outcomes must match the durable concern records.
2. The resolution has the expected reviewed schema and confirms two later agreeing observed claims with distinct source references and sensory channels. The original recorded observations genuinely disagreed.
3. Each corroborating assertion is cross-checked against a uniquely admitted Doctor world event with matching **event text, lived evidence class and nonexternal classification**. A forged or modified concern resolution alone cannot produce a verified episode.
4. Selection is based on the present subject-facing topic or a concern just resolved in the current scene. At most **two** episodes are selected, rather than dumping the conflict ledger.
5. The Doctor projects each chosen event as a first-person, factory-built `PhenomenalEvent` and `SubjectFrame` item. Only the subjective text reaches Synthetic Ipseity's existing publication and outbox. Internal concern IDs, evidence JSON, scores, source refs and timestamps remain on the engineer side.

The projected statement describes the evidence accurately, not as omniscient truth: *"I remember two later observations of the laboratory balance reading agreeing at 451 grams. Earlier readings disagreed. I regard that later agreement as stronger evidence, though it is not infallible."*

The preview renderer selection also deduplicates quoted User speech, retains at most **three** original inherited-identity preface lines chosen for role, vocation and metaphysical uncertainty, and caps the subject frame at **16 items** and **2,600 total item characters**. It preserves the original Doctor-produced typed items and the SubjectFrame factory boundary. These are preview-only selection rules, not changes to the neural controller, memory archive, policy weights, or normal production renderer.

### Test gates

The existing standalone Sensus, Doctor recall and Synthetic Ipseity suites remain in the preview workflow. New regression tests require:

- A vetoed measurement must not become subject-accessible as verified before corroboration.
- After corroboration, the real 451-gram resolution appears in the first-person frame immediately and again after process restart, without raw identifiers or the unverified 919-gram reading.
- A User message quote is not duplicated and identity text has a bounded share of the frame.
- An unrelated question does not force a resolved episode into the frame.
- A forged 777-gram concern resolution fails the source-content and lifecycle cross-check.
- The original five-scene ChatGPT proxy harness now treats the missing verified episode as a test failure, instead of preserving the original negative as an invariant.

The original v1 ChatGPT-authored response fixture is preserved. A separately labeled [v2 manually authored reply fixture](three-organ-chatgpt-proxy-responses-v2.json) tests whether the real Synthetic Ipseity callback can deliver grounded speech using the restored 451-gram episode after restart. **Both fixtures are unblinded, fixed-response replays, not fresh API-based model generations.** All automatic model-call counts remain zero.

## Remaining limitations

- These guards protect against incorrect or edited concern metadata under a trusted Doctor store. They are **not cryptographic protection against an actor who can rewrite the full SQLite database**.
- Distinct channels and source IDs are a conservative proxy, **not proof of independent sensor custody**. The standalone Sensus 80/160 object reidentification result remains unchanged.
- Topic matching is small lexical retrieval, not demonstrated long-horizon autonomous narrative understanding. Evidence absent from the subject frame cannot be recovered by a language model without hallucination.
- The natural-language phrasing of a subject recollection is a projection backed by evidence, not proof of phenomenological experience.
- The 13/18 endogenous-policy result vs simpler 14/18 rule remains a negative. We have not tuned the policy or claimed a model-quality win.
- CI tests run on a deterministic renderer plus manually prepared responses. Real Ollama versus ChatGPT parity, repeated sampling, blinded conversational quality and long-run identity drift are still open.
- The neural checkpoint/database recovery gap still fails closed in ambiguous crashes; the repair does not solve cross-database atomicity.

**Disposition:** Keep PR #59 draft and the Sensus donor untouched. Independent reviewer approval and a controlled live-model evaluation remain necessary before production promotion.
