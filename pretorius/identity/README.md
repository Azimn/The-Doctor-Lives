# Pretorius identity bootstrap

This directory will hold curated, provenance-bearing Pretorius seed material.

Seed classes:
- identity invariants;
- chronological autobiography;
- relationships and important people;
- formative experiences;
- intellectual/research history;
- values, preferences, aversions and recurring concerns;
- voice/behavior constraints;
- unresolved commitments or themes present at activation.

Rules:
1. Do not copy Calibos identity or memories.
2. Do not flatten the seed into one giant system prompt.
3. Separate historical/source evidence from later interpretation.
4. Renderer generations are not autobiographical facts merely because Pretorius said them.
5. New lived experience must carry provenance and may revise interpretations without silently rewriting history.
