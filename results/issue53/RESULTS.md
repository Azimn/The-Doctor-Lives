## Decisive negative results and unsupported claims

The strong simple nonneural controller completed the physical goal on an equivalent post-intervention world snapshot. All three successful recurrent-policy turns had a top abstract neural action that **did not match** the executed concrete action's mapped category. In all three, the ground-only relevance mapper would have selected the exact same action as the policy-weighted mapper. Therefore neural score ablation changed **zero of three** concrete decisions in this run.

The apparent agentic progression was driven by a narrow, condition-dependent and manually weighted affordance adapter. The test establishes a host/cognition persistence bridge, not independent learned object-level autonomy. The neural controller did execute and its scores were recorded, but its unique causal contribution to the selected tool actions was not demonstrated. State-dependent eligibility and the scripted book tasks should not be reported as independent action planning.

The no-episode condition removes the entire episode, including recurrent updates, so it is not a selective memory-only causal lesion. The foreign-memory control uses a hostile, direct trusted-store write in disposable state. Although its subject frame and eligible actions were protected at the adapter, private brain retrieval may still be influenced by that injected record; preventing direct trusted-store attacks is outside the current boundary. Neither control establishes a clean neurocognitive advantage.

There was no generalization suite, no independent holdout author, no blind behavioral adjudication, no external language-model rollout, and no long-horizon survival test. The test is insufficient for claims about open-ended autonomy, subjective consciousness, neural efficacy, or completeness as a living artificial character.

## Named blocking defect and next admission criterion

**Blocker GROUND-001:** The current production Pretorius policy outputs general-purpose tendencies such as approach, persist and explore, not goal-conditioned, object-and-operand decisions. The adapter currently supplies the decisive object-action relevance and history-based eligibility by deterministic rules. Under the observed no-neural-score ablation, the identical actions are selected. It would be misleading to promote this as autonomous action selection.

The next approved experiment under Issue #53 must keep the same lab authority and subject memory gates but test a controller that itself selects a specific legal action and operand from equally supplied eligible affordances. A genuine local language-model action selector or an appropriate existing model controller may serve as the challenger, provided its weights, data, prompts and actual executions are pinned and a strong simple baseline is held constant. A negative outcome should be retained as a negative outcome rather than patched with another speculative architecture. Independent holdout tasks and blind source adjudication are still required before claiming improved character continuity. No production merge until existing security, versioning, migration, rollback, and reviewer gates pass.
